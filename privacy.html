Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 July 2026

This policy explains what ListRight collects and why. ListRight is operated by Eric Antolin Diaz II, a sole proprietor based in Houston, Texas. Contact: support@listrightapp.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address — to create your account and sign you in. We use passwordless sign-in links, so we do not store a password.
  • Images you upload — photographs of trading cards, used to produce your listing output.
  • Usage records — how many cards you analysed, to enforce your plan limit.
  • Basic technical data — such as IP address and timestamps, kept for security.

We do not collect or store your payment card details. Payment is handled by Paddle, which acts as merchant of record. Card data never reaches our servers.

Why we use it

To provide the Service, to enforce plan limits, to contact you about your account, and to keep the Service secure. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary with the providers that operate the Service:

  • Paddle — payment processing and merchant of record
  • Anthropic — AI processing of uploaded card images to identify the card
  • Our hosting and email providers — to run the Service and send sign-in links

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is open. Uploaded images and their results are kept so you can revisit your own history. If you close your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we must retain records for legal or tax reasons.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correction of it, or deletion of it at any time by emailing support@listrightapp.com. If you are in the UK or EU, you have these rights under the GDPR, including the right to object to processing and to complain to your local supervisory authority. We respond within 30 days.

Cookies

We use a single essential cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

The Service is not directed to anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
